FIELD NOTES / TRAVEL THOUGHTFULLY

Good journeys
begin with care.

A few simple habits help make shared rides feel better for everyone.

✳
01 / BEFORE
⌖

Know the plan.

Confirm your pickup point, drop-off, departure time and cost before setting off.

02 / PEOPLE
◎

Trust, but verify.

Check who you are travelling with. Share trip details with someone you trust.

03 / ON THE ROAD
↗

Keep it respectful.

Agree on luggage, stops, music and seat arrangements. A little communication goes a long way.

04 / REMEMBER
✳

Your call matters.

If a journey doesn't feel right, don't get in. Prioritise your comfort and safety.

A note on shared rides

Confirm the vehicle, pickup point, departure time and cost before travelling. Wayvo is a student project; arrange rides thoughtfully and prioritise your safety.
